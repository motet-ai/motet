# Commercial Licensing

Motet uses a split license model:

- The runtime under `motet/` is available under the Functional Source License,
  Version 1.1, or a separate commercial license.
- The SDK under `motet-sdk/` is available under Apache License 2.0.

Private invite-only evaluation repositories, including `motet-eval`, are also subject to
the default [`EVALUATION_TERMS.md`](EVALUATION_TERMS.md) unless a separate signed
agreement says otherwise.

## When FSL Is Usually Enough

The FSL runtime license is intended to allow evaluation, internal use, modification for
internal use, non-commercial education/research, and building products or workflows on
top of Motet, provided the use is not a competing commercial product or service.

## When To Discuss Commercial Terms

Contact Motet for commercial terms if you need rights or commitments beyond the public
FSL grant, such as:

- Offering Motet or substantially similar functionality as a hosted or managed service.
- Redistributing Motet as part of a commercial product where the FSL competing-use
  restriction is unclear.
- Enterprise procurement terms, warranty, indemnity, support SLAs, or private deployment
  assistance.
- Source review, pilot access, or prospect evaluation terms under a separate agreement.
- Additional architecture materials beyond the curated public ADR set, when shared under
  NDA, pilot, or commercial evaluation terms.
