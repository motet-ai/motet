# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Motet to be a respectful and productive space for developers, users, customers,
and contributors. Participants are expected to communicate constructively, assume good
intent where reasonable, and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and patient in issues, discussions, pull requests, and support channels.
- Give actionable feedback and provide context when reporting problems.
- Respect project maintainers' time and decisions.
- Avoid sharing private customer, security, or business-sensitive information in public
  channels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Disruptive behavior that makes collaboration harder.
- Bad-faith use of project channels for spam, abuse, or competitive intelligence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, restrict access, or take other
reasonable action to protect the project and community. Security-sensitive or private
conduct concerns can be sent to `security@motet.dev`.
